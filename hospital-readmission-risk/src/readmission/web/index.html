Hospital Readmission Risk Prediction

AI/ML Study Project: from hospital data to a working prediction demo

Estimates the risk that a hospital stay for a patient with diabetes is followed by a readmission within 30 days, using information known at discharge. It shows how a care team could decide which discharged patients to prioritize for follow-up review.

Column guide

Required columns must be present. Medication columns are optional and default to "No". The dataset's own markers "?" (not recorded) and "None" (not measured) are accepted.
